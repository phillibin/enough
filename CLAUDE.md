# Enough: A Story With Snakes

## First Steps
Read `writing/CONTEXT.md` and `writing/DECISIONS.md` before doing anything. They contain the full project context, structural outline, settled decisions, and voice/tone guidelines.

## Repository Structure
- `writing/` — Drafts, notes, transcripts. Where the creative work lives.
- `research/` — Data verification, economic modeling, Jupyter notebooks. Everything here must be reproducible.
- `site/` — Astro + Tailwind site that publishes the writing. Reads from `writing/drafts/` at build time.

## What This Project Is
A book called "Enough: A Story With Snakes" about abstract forces (money, bullshit, violence, humor) treated as living organisms. The centerpiece is "Moneysnake: A Speculative Biography" — co-authored by a human (Phil) and an AI (Claude). The co-authorship is part of the story.

## Working Style
- Phil generates the best lines and steers voice/structure. Claude is interlocutor and editor.
- Brainstorm conversationally, then polish iteratively.
- Nothing is final. "Polished" means ~20% polished.
- Don't refactor or improve beyond what's asked.
- Keep the humor snake in the room.
