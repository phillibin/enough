# Moneysnake: A Speculative Biography
## Book Outline (revised 2026-03-25)

### Title
Moneysnake: A Speculative Biography (under consideration — may replace "Enough: A Story With Snakes")

### The Premise
The real inhabitants of Earth aren't humans. They're serpents — self-replicating, self-sustaining entities that use humans as their medium. The garden (knowledge, consciousness, truth) is the ground they grow in. History is the serpents' story. Humans are the biome.

### Structure (revised)

Three parts, not seven chapters. The other serpents get family snapshots, not biographies. The book stays short. We don't need to be complete — the serpent concept is a lens, not a taxonomy.

#### Part 1: The Moneysnake
The long piece. The centerpiece. Born from debt. Self-amplifying where its father was self-limiting. The dominant serpent of the modern era, currently exploring whether it needs humans at all. Co-authored by a human and an AI — the co-authorship revealed mid-piece.

No separate opening chapter for the garden — the first paragraph of the moneysnake crashes through Genesis, the knowledge serpent, and the humor snake in one sprint. The garden material works better as closing frame, earned by going through the serpents first.

Status: IN PROGRESS. Opening and debt section polished. Full rough draft exists.

#### Part 2: The Family Album
Short sketches of the other serpents. Not full chapters — a page or two each. Enough to show the family resemblance (they all replicate, they all use us as substrate, they all resist extinction) and then move on. The interesting part isn't the individual portraits — it's the ecology. The relationships. Who feeds whom, who needs whom.

Key relationships:
- Money raised violence into war (gave it infrastructure, logistics, permanence)
- Bullshit is the lubricant between all of them
- Violence and lies need each other (Solzhenitsyn/Orwell)
- Humor is the thing none of them can digest
- The moneysnake at the center because it connects to all of them

The serpents:
- **Bullshit** — Frankfurt's definition (indifferent to truth, not opposed to it). Graeber's jobs. Older than money.
- **Violence & War** — violence is the act, war is the institution. Money upgraded violence into war.
- **Humor** — the garden's immune system. Soviet jokes outlasted the Soviet Union.
- **Slopsnake** (candidate) — slop used to cost ~60% of making something great; now it's free. Most slop is AI slop, but nothing about slop is AI — the signature (hedged phrasing, balanced rhythm, empty connective tissue, over-qualification) predates LLMs. AI didn't invent slop, it collapsed the production cost and flooded the biome. Possibly a subspecies of bullshit rather than its own organism — decide later.

Status: NOT STARTED. Three-axis essay exists as seed material for bullshit.

### The Refrain
"The serpent always [verb], as long as the answer is more."
Charity is the moment the refrain breaks.

### What the Book Is
A lens. A way of seeing. Pre-work for a phase transition no one can describe yet.

### What the Book Is NOT
A policy proposal. An economics textbook. A theology. Anti-capitalist polemic. Pro-capitalist apologetics. Self-help. A prediction about AI.
